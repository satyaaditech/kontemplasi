*Eradicating the Illusion of Kumingsun: NPD and the Path of the True Guide 🧘✨*

_Sugeng Enjang, Salam Karahayon_

*📖 DAILY SCRIPTURE* :

Sasangka Jati, Serat Panembah: _"Isih ana saprakara maneh kang ginawe nyaring kasucianing sahadatira, yaiku nyingkirana sakabehing watak kang nuntun marang adigang, adigung, adiguna utawa gumunggung, sarta dhemen pangaji-aji lan suwur, sesongaran, piangkuh, lan sapanunggalane. Nanging sira uga kudu mangerti marang ajining dhirinira, mulane aja nyenyamah dhiri tumiba ing panggawe nguthuh. Dene dedalan kang prayoga ginawe nanggulangi (ngilangake) ambek kumaluhur (gumedhe), iku panalangsa lan kalakuan andhap asor (susila anoraga)."_

Translation: _"There is still one more thing used to filter the purity of your testimony (shahadat), which is to avoid all character traits that lead to boasting of strength, power, and cleverness (adigang, adigung, adiguna) or self-exaltation, and the desire for validation and fame (dhemen pangaji-aji lan suwur), showing off, arrogance, and so on. But you must also understand your own self-worth, so do not degrade yourself into despicable actions (nguthuh). The proper way to overcome (eradicate) the arrogant/grandiose character (gumedhe) is penance (panalangsa) and the practice of humility (andhap asor/susila anoraga)."_

💭 *EXPOSITION* :

There is a profound correlation between the phenomenon of Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD) in modern psychology and the spiritual obstacle known as *kumingsun* in the teachings of Sang Guru Sejati. NPD is characterized by a grandiose sense of self-importance (kumaluhur), an excessive need for admiration (dhemen pangaji-aji lan suwur), and a lack of empathy toward others. In this state, the mind (angen-angen) and desires (hawa nafsu) hijack both the body and batin (inner self), causing one to lose touch with the True Guide (Tuntunan Jati). 🕊️

Sasangka Jati explains that the root of this *kumingsun* behavior is when the soul (jiwa) is dominated by the lower self (Pribadi Asor / lager-zelf). The mind (angen-angen), when governed by the fire element (anasir latu), has a tendency to exalt that which is not the true Self as the supreme Self (*ngingsun-ingsunaken ingkang sanes Ingsun*). In other words, the individual considers their ego or false self to be the center of the universe (pepunthaning jagad), deviating from the path of virtue and falling into arrogance (adigang-adigung-adiguna).

Nonetheless, Sang Guru Sejati does not want us to develop low self-esteem or self-loathing (*nyenyamah dhiri*). We must maintain a healthy understanding of our self-worth so that we do not fall into despair or nista (nguthuh). The spiritual therapy to heal this *kumingsun* character is through the practice of *panalangsa* (deep, honest penance/surrender in the presence of the Lord) and cultivating *andhap asor* (humility/susila anoraga). *Panalangsa* is the act of sincerely acknowledging all our limitations and weaknesses (mental and physical) before the Divine.

When we practice *panalangsa* and humility, the rigid defense mechanism of the lower ego dissolves. The soul submits once again to the guidance of Suksma Sejati through the practice of Tri Sila, which is mindfulness (sadar/eling), faith (percaya/pracaya), and obedience (mituhu/taat). In this way, the spiritual illness of *kumingsun* can be healed, allowing the soul to achieve everlasting peace.

🧘 *DAILY PRACTICE* :

Practicing the control of the grandiose ego and nurturing humility:

1. When our desires or opinions are contested or not agreed upon by others, pause and calm our breath, reminding ourselves that we are not the ultimate arbiter of truth. 🌱
2. In every good deed, realign our inner intention that it is done purely as an offering to God, thereby freeing ourselves from the longing to be praised or validated by others. 🌾
3. Cultivate honest self-examination (niti priksa dhiri) every night before sleeping, admitting our shortcomings and surrendering ourselves to the purifying guidance of Suksma Sejati.

🎯 *REFLEXION* :

The grandiose ego (kumingsun) is a spiritual shackle that veils our union with the Divine, and it can be dissolved through penance (panalangsa) and genuine humility.

  Are we willing to sincerely acknowledge our limitations and practice penance (panalangsa) in the presence of the Lord?
