*📖 SABDA OF THE DAY :*

Serat Sasangka Jati, Chapter III (Kawruh Kasampurnan - Hasta Sila No. 3, Temen) : _"Temen iku tegese kang baku: netepi janji utawa netepi sesanggeman, sanadyan wus kelair ing wicara utawa isih kabatin (niyat) iya padha wae. Dadi wong kang ora netepi niyate, iku jenenge nyidrani karo batine dhewe, dene yen niyat mau wus kelair ing wicara, mangka ora ditetepi, iku gorohe mau ateges diseksekake marang wong liya. Padha sinaua marang katemenaning atinira, amarga temen iku nekakake marang adil, dene adil iku nuntun marang kamulyan langgeng. Temen iku maweh kakendelan, sarta katentreman marang ati, lan uga nucekake ati, apa dene agawe tulusing pambekan. Wong ora bisa nglakoni agama kalawan becik, manawa atine ora suci, dene ati ora bisa suci, manawa ilate uga ora suci. Kang tetep marang bener, sanadyan katemenanira mau bisa agawe kapitunanira, lan aja sok dhemen goroh, nadyan gorohira mau bisa agawe kauntunganira."_

Translation : _"Temen (truthfulness/integrity/faithfulness) fundamentally means: fulfilling promises and commitments, whether they have been spoken aloud or remain held within the inner heart (intention), both carry the same weight. Thus, one who does not fulfill their inner intention betrays their own inner soul; and if that intention has been spoken in words yet remains unfulfilled, that falsehood is witnessed by others. Learn to cultivate the truthfulness of your heart, because integrity brings forth justice, and justice leads toward eternal glory. Truthfulness grants true courage, brings peace to the heart, purifies the heart, and fosters sincerity in noble character. One cannot practice true virtue well if the heart is not pure, and the heart cannot be pure if the tongue is not pure. Stand firm upon the truth, even if your truthfulness may bring apparent loss; and never delight in falsehood, even if that falsehood may bring temporary gain."_

💭 *EXPOSITION :*

Have you ever experienced a lingering sense of unease throughout the day, simply because of one small thing: making a promise you secretly knew you could not keep? Or perhaps, setting a sincere intention in the morning to start a wholesome habit, only to find convenient excuses to abandon it when the moment arrives?

A timeless story of a classical master watchmaker offers a profound insight. One day, an apprentice asked why the master spent hours meticulously polishing a tiny gear buried deep inside the clock mechanism—a hidden component that no customer would ever see. The master smiled gently and replied: *"The buyer may never see it, but God and my conscience see it clearly."*

This is the very essence of the virtue *Temen* taught by Sang Guru Sejati. Integrity is not merely about managing our public reputation; it is the absolute harmony between the quiet intentions of our inner heart and the actions we manifest in the world. As Warren Buffett wisely noted, it takes decades to build a reputation of integrity, but only minutes of compromise with falsehood to destroy it.

When we tell a lie or offer hollow promises for immediate advantage, our own inner soul bears the initial burden. We become trapped in hidden anxiety: fearing exposure, struggling to keep deceptive stories consistent, and gradually eroding our own self-respect. Conversely, when we courageously uphold *Temen*—even when it seems disadvantageous in worldly terms—our soul is rewarded with something priceless: *kakendelan* (unshakable inner freedom and courage) and *katentreman* (true peace of mind). A truthful heart never fears shadows, because it walks in the pure light of Sang Guru Sejati. 🌿🕊️

🧘 *PRACTICE :*

Quiet steps to nurture the virtue of Temen today:
1. Reflect upon small commitments made to family, colleagues, or yourself; fulfill them wholeheartedly or communicate transparently without delay. ⏰
2. When facing difficult choices at work or in daily life, choose honesty and accountability, even when a convenient lie seems to offer an easier path. 🛡️
3. Before speaking or sending messages on social platforms, pause briefly to ensure your words are genuine and truthful, free from pretense or empty flattery. 🕯️

🎯 *CONTEMPLATION :*

One who is truthful to their inner soul needs no masks before the world, for genuine peace always blooms from a clear conscience.

📝 *APPLICATION :*

1. What sincere promise or inner intention have we been postponing or neglecting within ourselves? 💭
2. Do we have the courage to choose truth and integrity today, even if it requires letting go of temporary comfort or fleeting gains?
3. How can we align our daily words and deeds more closely with the silent guidance of Sang Guru Sejati?

🙏 May we all remain in the Loving Protection and Guidance of Sang Guru Sejati. Satuhu. 🙏
