## *PANGESTU CONTEMPLATION*

### ⚖️🕯️ *TEMEN: The Foundation of Inner Integrity and True Peace* 🛡️🕊️✨

_Warm greetings to all Brothers and Sisters,_

#### *📖 SABDA OF THE DAY :*

Serat Sasangka Jati, Chapter III (Kawruh Kasampurnan - Hasta Sila No. 3, Temen) : _"Temen iku tegese kang baku: netepi janji utawa netepi sesanggeman, sanadyan wus kelair ing wicara utawa isih kabatin (niyat) iya padha wae. Dadi wong kang ora netepi niyate, iku jenenge nyidrani karo batine dhewe, dene yen niyat mau wus kelair ing wicara, mangka ora ditetepi, iku gorohe mau ateges diseksekake marang wong liya. Padha sinaua marang katemenaning atinira, amarga temen iku nekakake marang adil, dene adil iku nuntun marang kamulyan langgeng. Temen iku maweh kakendelan, sarta katentreman marang ati, lan uga nucekake ati, apa dene agawe tulusing pambekan. Wong ora bisa nglakoni agama kalawan becik, manawa atine ora suci, dene ati ora bisa suci, manawa ilate uga ora suci. Kang tetep marang bener, sanadyan katemenanira mau bisa agawe kapitunanira, lan aja sok dhemen goroh, nadyan gorohira mau bisa agawe kauntunganira."_

Translation : _"Temen (truthfulness/integrity/faithfulness) fundamentally means: fulfilling promises and commitments, whether they have been spoken aloud or remain held within the inner heart (intention), both carry the same weight. Thus, one who does not fulfill their inner intention betrays their own inner soul; and if that intention has been spoken in words yet remains unfulfilled, that falsehood is witnessed by others. Learn to cultivate the truthfulness of your heart, because integrity brings forth justice, and justice leads toward eternal glory. Truthfulness grants true courage, brings peace to the heart, purifies the heart, and fosters sincerity in noble character. One cannot practice true virtue well if the heart is not pure, and the heart cannot be pure if the tongue is not pure. Stand firm upon the truth, even if your truthfulness may bring apparent loss; and never delight in falsehood, even if that falsehood may bring temporary gain."_

#### 💭 *EXPOSITION :*

In today's fast-paced and competitive world, our integrity is constantly challenged by the allure of instant advantages or the fear of external judgment. 📱 In professional spheres and digital social spaces, people often feel pressured to make hollow promises, mask shortcomings behind deceptive appearances, or adopt superficial pretenses to protect their public image. Yet, every falsehood committed—even if it garners outward praise—invariably plants seeds of tension, guilt, and restlessness within the inner conscience.

The teachings of Sang Guru Sejati in Serat Sasangka Jati place the virtue of *Temen* as one of the fundamental pillars of Hasta Sila. Temen transcends mere outward compliance; it represents absolute alignment with our inner truth and conscience. When we harbor a noble intention but abandon it out of convenience, we fracture our own inner peace. Conversely, when our words and actions remain faithful to truth, the soul is endowed with *"kakendelan"* (genuine inner fortitude without fear) and profound serenity that surpasses worldly calculations of profit and loss. 🌿

Inner integrity cleanses the mind from the turbulence of selfish desires and anxious thoughts. While walking the path of truth may occasionally seem disadvantageous by worldly metrics, purity of character will always guide the seeker into genuine justice and enduring peace under the loving guidance of Sang Guru Sejati. 🕊️

#### 🧘 *PRACTICE :*

Daily practice to nurture the virtue of Temen:
1. Begin the day by examining the harmony between inner intentions and outer speech; when making a commitment to yourself or others, fulfill it diligently without procrastination. ⏰
2. Courageously uphold honesty and accountability in every action, resisting the temptation of convenient falsehoods for temporary gains. 🛡️
3. Guard the speech from gossip, deceitful promises, and hypocrisy, allowing the inner heart to remain unburdened and serene in its devotion to the Divine. 🕯️

✅ Practicable anywhere and at any time.

#### 🎯 *CONTEMPLATION :*

Inner integrity is the true sanctuary of unconditional peace, for a truthful heart is never troubled by the shadows of deception.

#### 📝 *APPLICATION :*

1. Are our spoken words and promises today in complete alignment with the sincere intentions of our inner heart? 💭
2. How do we respond when the temptation of falsehood offers immediate ease or short-term advantage?
3. Have we anchored our trust firmly in eternal truth, knowing that Divine protection transcends all worldly gains?

🙏 May we all remain in the Loving Protection and Guidance of Sang Guru Sejati. Satuhu. 🙏
